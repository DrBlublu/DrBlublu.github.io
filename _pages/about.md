I am a cognitive scientist that stepped too far away from the light, wandered into anthropology. That lead me to study cultural evolution from a cognitive standpoint. MY research focus on technological evolution (how did we make those tools?), social learning (how can I learn from others?) and cuisine evolution (I just like food). My work comprise mainly of computational modelling, agent-based models, behavioural experiments and large-scale cultural data.


Research
======
I'm currently focusing of cuisine and food evolution. One of my projects investigates the evolution of spice usage in French cuisine over time, using a large-scale recipe dataset.

Another project focuses on modern-day data and explores the relationship between spice usage and health-related benefits. More specifically, I hypothesise that the potential health benefits of spices may have played a role in shaping their use in cuisine.

I'm also interested in the cognitive and cultural mechanisms underlying innovation. I investigate these processes using agent-based models, which I validate against data on recipe innovation.
